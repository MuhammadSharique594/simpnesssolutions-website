Retail

Solutions for retail businesses.

Retail complexity is rarely about the storefront. It is about stock, locations, suppliers, and the delay between a sale and a decision.

Common problems

  • 01
    POS, inventory, and purchasing that do not share a single picture of the truth.
  • 02
    Multi-location stock that is counted too late to prevent a stockout.
  • 03
    Promotions and replenishment that still live in spreadsheets.

Where technology helps

Inventory systems, POS integration, internal operations software, and demand signals that surface exceptions before they become a crisis. AI is useful here when it flags what a buyer should look at — not when it pretends to run the store.
